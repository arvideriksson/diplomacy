Country Badge Concepts — Diplomacy, 1901

Six minimalistic badge systems for the seven Great Powers. Every emblem is correct for 1901: France is the Third Republic (no royal fleur-de-lis), Italy & Russia & Austria are kingdoms/empires under their crowns, Germany is the Kaiserreich, Turkey is the Ottoman Empire. Colors reuse the in-app palette.

1 · Monogram roundels

Flat colored disc, single initial. The most compact, scales to a pin.

2 · Heraldic shields

Minimal escutcheon, era-correct charge. Reads as a coat of arms without the clutter.

3 · Symbol glyphs

One iconic 1901 emblem per power, stroked line-art in the power color.

4 · Flag roundels

The 1901 national flag clipped into a circle. Instantly recognizable, no text.

5 · Solid glyph chips

Filled color disc, knocked-out white symbol. High contrast, great on a busy map.

6 · Stripe tabs

Rounded tab, flag stripe + initial. Works inline next to a player name.
